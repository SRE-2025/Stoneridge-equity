01 Why Stoneridge

Get in on the ground floor of something built to endure.

Most people join a firm. Here, you help build one. We're small by design and early by choice — which means real ownership of your work, a seat close to every decision, and the rare chance to shape a firm's culture from the start.

We back founders taking companies to the next level. We look for the same instinct in the people we hire: builders who would rather own an outcome than manage a process, and who measure themselves by what lasts.

02 What it's like here

Fewer people. More ownership.

Real ownership

You'll own workstreams outright, not fetch slides for someone who does. Small team, big surface area.

Operator DNA

We've built and run companies. You'll learn how value is actually created, not just modeled in a spreadsheet.

A seat at the table

Close to founders, deals, and decisions from day one — the exposure you'd wait years for elsewhere.

Built to last

Rooted in Austin

Aligned incentives

We win when the companies we back win — and we build the team's rewards around the same idea.
