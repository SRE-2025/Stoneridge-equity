01 The Firm

We do one thing, and we intend to do it exceptionally well.

Stoneridge Equity was founded in 2016 by a group of operators and investors who had spent their careers building companies — and grew tired of watching good businesses passed between owners chasing the next quarter.

We set out to build the opposite: a firm patient enough to hold, disciplined enough to say no, and experienced enough to genuinely help. Every partner invests personally in every fund. We answer to exactly the outcomes our partners do.

Read our story

02 What defines us

Four principles we do not compromise.

01

Alignment above all

Every partner invests personally in each fund. When our interests and our partners' are inseparable, the right decision is usually the obvious one.

02

A longer horizon

We are built to hold — through expansions, downturns, and the years between. Compounding rewards patience, and we have structured a firm designed to wait.

03

Operators, not spectators

We have founded, scaled, and led companies. We bring that lived experience to the boardroom — as partners to management, never as passengers.

04

Integrity as strategy

Reputation is the one asset that cannot be recapitalized. We do what we say, we say what is true, and we treat every counterparty as a future partner.

04 How we partner

More than capital.
An extended team.

We don't hand over a check and wait for the board meeting. We bring operators who have done the job, and we roll up our sleeves where it moves the needle.

Operators who have sat in the chair

Our operating partners have founded companies, run them, and lived through the hard quarters. They join boards, mentor leadership, and step in on go-to-market, talent, and systems.
