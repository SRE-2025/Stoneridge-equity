01 Our story

Founded on conviction.

Stoneridge Equity began with a group of operators and investors who had spent their careers building companies — and grew frustrated watching good businesses passed between owners chasing the next quarter.

We set out to create the opposite: a firm patient enough to hold, disciplined enough to say no, and experienced enough to genuinely help. From day one, every partner has invested personally in every fund. We answer to the same outcomes our partners do.

Today, from our home in Austin, we partner with lower-middle-market companies across North America. We remain intentionally small — because the quality of our judgment, not the size of our assets, is what compounds.

03 What we value

The character behind the capital.

Integrity

We do what we say. Every counterparty is treated as a future partner, because in this business they usually are.

Patience

We measure success in decades. Our structure and our temperament are both built to hold through the cycle.

Discipline

We pass on far more than we pursue. Saying no to the good is how we stay available for the exceptional.

Partnership

We are collaborators, not conquerors. Management teams keep their seats — and gain a genuine ally.

Ambition

Patience is not passivity. We help good companies become great ones — and great ones become enduring.

Stewardship

We inherit companies built by others. We treat them — their people and their legacy — with the care that deserves.

04 Our home

Rooted in Austin. Investing across America.

Austin taught us that the most interesting companies aren't always on the coasts. From the center of the country, we see the whole of it.

Our location keeps us close to a generation of founders, operators, and industries building real things. It also keeps us grounded — in a culture that values substance over spectacle, exactly like the businesses we back.
